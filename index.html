Privacy Policy for Charge Doctor

Effective Date: October 2026

Charge Doctor ("we", "our", or "the app") provides real-time battery hardware diagnostics and health metrics. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through complete transparency.

Information Collection and Use

Charge Doctor does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, unique device identifiers, or location information.

The app accesses hardware battery metrics (such as battery percentage, voltage, temperature, charge rate, and power flow) locally via standard Android operating system APIs solely to display live gauges and health calculations within the app interface. All data remains exclusively on your physical device and is never transmitted to external servers.

In-App Purchases

Optional upgrades (such as Pro features) are processed directly by Google Play In-App Billing. We do not receive, process, or store your credit card or financial details. Transactions are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via developer support contact.
